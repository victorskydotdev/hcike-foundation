Who We Are

Care Beyond Boundaries

HCIKE Foundation is a non-governmental organisation dedicated to transforming lives by delivering essential health services to those who need them most.

Our Vision

To create a healthier future for rural communities by providing sustainable healthcare solutions and empowering local healthcare providers.

Our Mission

To provide free medical outreach and academic scholarships to the underprivileged in rural Nigeria and across Africa through comprehensive programs and strategic partnerships.

What Drives Us

Our core values

Five principles that guide every decision, every outreach, every life we touch.

01

Compassion

Delivering empathetic and respectful care to every patient we serve, without exception.

02

Ensuring consistently high standards in all healthcare services and educational programs.

03

04
